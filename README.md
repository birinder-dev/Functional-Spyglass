# 🔍 Functional Spyglass

![Functional Spyglass Banner](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/ecc8154670f742bdaba97c361759929907ee2a03.jpeg)

**Functional Spyglass** turns your standard Minecraft Spyglass into a tactical client-side inspection HUD! Inspect live **3D face portraits**, exact **health & Effective Health (EHP) math**, **armor reduction**, **mount speed/jump stats**, and **active potion effects** for any mob, animal, creature, or player across any server.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Screenshots

### 🖼️ 3D Live Face Portraits & Player Inspection
Displays an ARK-style 3D portrait plaque with universal eye-anchored matrix centering (zero world mob lag or walking stutter). Works on all mobs, animals, and other players on multiplayer servers!

![Player & Mob Inspection](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/51782dabc48d72558f754cfca99dfaf649212277.jpeg)

---

### 🐎 Mount Stats Scanner
Scope horses, donkeys, and mules to display exact movement speed (`m/s`) and jump height (`blocks`) before taming or breeding!

![Zombie Horse Statistics](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c7afdb7b2c95601eb264277c354b6c35b5c62872.jpeg)

### 🔮 Threat-Colored Spectral Outlines
Highlights targeted creatures with dynamic glowing colors:
* 🌸 **Pink:** Tamed Pets & Mounts (`★ Tamed`)
* 🔴 **Red:** Aggressive Mobs & Monsters
* ⚪ **White:** Neutral Creatures
* 🟢 **Green:** Passive Animals & Villagers

![Enderman Threat Outline](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/41af3d723eab228812c3d70af417c2e96588f1b3.jpeg)

### 🔍 3-Step Scroll Zoom
Use your mouse scroll wheel while scoping to switch between `0.5x` (panoramic), `1.0x` (standard), and `2.0x` (deep zoom) without switching hotbar slots!

![0.5x vs 2.0x Zoom Comparison](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/9c7aab0081cde8e43198d5546fd129a02c2a990b.jpeg)

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Put a **Spyglass** in your hand or off-hand.
2. Hold **Right-Click** to scope at any creature, mob, or player up to 64 blocks away.
3. Use the **Mouse Scroll Wheel** to adjust your zoom level (`0.5x`, `1.0x`, `2.0x`).
